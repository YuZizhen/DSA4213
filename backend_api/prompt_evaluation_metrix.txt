Quality of Questions:
-Relevance: Measure the relevance of the generated questions to the provided context or input.
-Clarity: Assess the clarity of the questions in terms of language fluency, grammatical correctness, and coherence.

Correctness of Answers:
-Accuracy: Determine the accuracy of the correct answers provided by the algorithm.

Difficulty Level:
-Appropriateness: Assess whether the difficulty level of the questions aligns with university student standard.
-Balanced Difficulty: (If possible) Ensure a balanced distribution of easy, medium, and hard questions.

Novelty and Creativity:
-Uniqueness: Evaluate the uniqueness of the generated questions compared to previous generated ones.
-Creativity: Measure the algorithm's ability to generate creative and thought-provoking questions.

Bias and Fairness:
-Bias Detection: Identify and mitigate biases in question generation, such as cultural biases or gender biases.
-Fairness: (If possible) Ensure fairness in question distribution across different topics, subjects, or demographics.

Robustness and Error Handling:
-Error Tolerance: This is not prompt evaluation. Ensure timeout errors are handdled and all the input collections have valid contents for MCQ generation.
